Ashgrove Community Energy · Board Pack · Q3 20262

Operations report

Generation from the three rooftop arrays was 412 MWh for the quarter, 6% above forecast, helped by a dry August. The Hollins Road inverter fault in July cost an estimated 9 MWh before it was replaced under warranty.

Member numbers rose to 1,138. The share offer for the school roofs project closed on 30 September at £286,000 against a £300,000 target; the Board is asked to decide at item 5 whether to proceed with a reduced scope.
