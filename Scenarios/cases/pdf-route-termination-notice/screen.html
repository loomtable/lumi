Assured Shorthold Tenancy Agreement · 14 Alder Row, Flat 32

Part B — Rent, deposit and bills

3.1The Tenant shall pay rent of £1,325.00 per calendar month in advance on the 1st day of each month by standing order to the account set out in Schedule 2.
3.2The fixed term of this Agreement is twelve (12) months beginning on 1 November 2026. What happens at the end of the fixed term, and how either party may bring this Agreement to an end, is set out in clause 17 (Ending the tenancy).
3.3The Landlord may review the rent once in any twelve-month period by giving the Tenant not less than one month's written notice of the new rent.
4.1On signing, the Tenant shall pay a deposit of £1,528.00, equal to five weeks' rent, which the Landlord shall protect with an approved tenancy deposit scheme within thirty (30) days of receipt.
4.2The deposit shall be returned within ten (10) days of the parties agreeing any deductions, as described in clause 18 (Check-out and deductions).
5.1The Tenant is responsible for council tax, gas, electricity, water, broadband and the television licence for the duration of the tenancy.
5.2The Tenant shall notify each utility supplier of the start date of the tenancy and provide meter readings to the Landlord at check-in.
6.1The Tenant shall not keep any animal at the Property without the Landlord's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

Landlord: Marrow & Finch Lettings Ltd  ·  Tenant: Jonah Eastwick
