7. Term and renewal

7.1This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an Initial Term of twelve (12) months.
7.2Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms of twenty-four (24) months each unless the Customer gives written notice of non-renewal no less than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the then-current term.

8. Fees and payment

8.1The Customer shall pay the Fees set out in Schedule 2 within thirty (30) days of the date of invoice.
8.2The Provider may increase the Fees at any time on fifteen (15) days’ written notice. Continued use of the Services after the notice period constitutes acceptance of the increased Fees.
8.3Late payments accrue interest at four percent (4%) per month until paid in full.

9. Liability

9.1Nothing in this Agreement limits or excludes liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation.
9.2Subject to clause 9.1, the Provider’s total aggregate liability arising under or in connection with this Agreement shall not exceed the Fees paid by the Customer in the one (1) month preceding the event giving rise to the claim.
9.3The Customer shall indemnify the Provider against all losses, costs, damages and expenses, without limit, arising from any use of the Services by the Customer or its personnel.

10. Customer data

10.1The Customer grants the Provider a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free licence to use Customer Data, in anonymised or aggregated form, for any purpose.
10.2The Provider shall process personal data only on the documented instructions of the Customer and in accordance with the Data Processing Addendum at Schedule 4.
