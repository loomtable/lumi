21. Force majeure

21.1In this clause, a “Force Majeure Event” means any event or circumstance beyond the reasonable control of the party affected, including without limitation the events listed in Schedule 5, but excluding any lack of funds or the insolvency of either party.
21.2Neither party shall be liable for any delay in or failure to perform its obligations under this Lease to the extent that the delay or failure is caused by a Force Majeure Event, provided that the affected party notifies the other party in writing within ten (10) Business Days of becoming aware of it.
21.3Nothing in this clause shall relieve the Tenant of the obligation to pay the Rent, the Service Charge or any other sum due under this Lease, which shall continue to fall due on the dates set out in clause 4.
21.4If a Force Majeure Event prevents the use of the Premises for a continuous period of more than ninety (90) days, either party may terminate this Lease by giving not less than thirty (30) days’ written notice to the other.

22. Notices

22.1Any notice under this Lease shall be in writing and delivered by hand or sent by recorded delivery to the address of the recipient set out at the start of this Lease, or to such other address as the recipient may notify.
