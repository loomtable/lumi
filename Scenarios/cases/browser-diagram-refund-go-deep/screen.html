Refunds and webhooks

A refund moves through several states. Create one with POST /v2/refunds against a captured charge; Tollgate replies 202 Accepted and the refund starts as pending.

Refund states

StateMeaningWebhook sent
pendingAccepted, waiting for the card networkrefund.created
succeededFunds returned to the customer (1–5 business days)refund.succeeded
failedCard closed or expired; balance credited back to yourefund.failed
requires_actionBank transfer refunds need the customer's IBANrefund.requires_action

Handling events

Your endpoint must return a 2xx within 10 seconds. Failed deliveries are retried with exponential backoff for up to 72 hours. Verify the Tollgate-Signature header before trusting any payload.

Partial refunds create one refund object each. A charge is marked refunded only when the refunded total equals the captured amount. See Disputes for how refunds interact with chargebacks.
