Harlow Civic Tower

From OpenAtlas, the free architecture encyclopedia

The Harlow Civic Tower is a 19-storey Brutalist office block in the fictional town of Harlow Vale, completed in 1971 to designs by the firm Denholm & Pryce. It is known for its board-marked béton brut concrete, deep-set ribbon windows and the cantilevered council chamber that projects over the entrance plaza.

The tower's stepped upper floors and chunky service cores are often compared with other civic buildings of the period. Critics in the 1980s called it "a filing cabinet for people", but it was listed for protection in 2019 after a public campaign.

The exterior was cleaned and the original timber window frames restored in 2023. The ground-floor plaza now hosts a weekly market.
