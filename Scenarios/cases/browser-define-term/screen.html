Central banks keep shrinking their balance sheets

The Federal Reserve said it will continue quantitative tightening through the first quarter, letting about $60 billion of Treasuries mature each month without reinvesting the proceeds. Analysts said the pace could slow if money-market rates rise above the policy range, as they did briefly in September.

Bond yields rose 6 basis points after the announcement. The European Central Bank is running a similar programme, though it stopped reinvestments earlier in 2025.
